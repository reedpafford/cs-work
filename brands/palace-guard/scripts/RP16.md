## Memo
Why: L4 liquidation, a short second ask for everyone who watched our ads this fall and didn't buy; the shape is the Onuia warehouse ad from Reed's strat-call folder ("02 - BOF Banker," 53 seconds), and Black Friday week is a clock we can actually use. · What: the Palace Guard team, handheld in the packing room, packing holiday orders: the boss approved the best deal we've ever run so grandmas and moms can get one in her stocking before she goes back to school in January, and it ends when these shelves are empty. · How: Onuia's banker (a selfie walk through the warehouse, four staff passing the phone, the product held up in every shot, the offer said three times in three mouths, the boss as the scarcity, an end card); RP9's stocking line and RP14's "before she goes back after the break"; the offer lines from RP13 and RP17.
Avatar: mothers and grandmothers 45 to 70 who already know what Palace Guard is · Sub-avatar: the mom or grandma shopping for Christmas who meant to put one on her girl's keys after the last ad and didn't; the daughter or granddaughter is home for winter break and heads back in January.
Desire: a gift that means something, she hands it over at Christmas and knows her girl goes back to school protected, at the lowest price she'll ever see (and one for every girl in the family).
Angle: the holiday gift with a deadline she already feels; she's home for the break, she goes back in January, and this is the cheapest it will ever be to send her back with one.
Mechanism: none new; one line of what it is (130 dB, the siren that gets everyone looking) for the one who forgot.
Authority: the people who pack them; the boss is the detective who makes them.
Route: format, the BOF banker (new to this account).

## V1 - Grandmas
### Text Hook
GRANDMAS are BUYING these for CHRISTMAS…🎁
### Script
(handheld selfie, walking fast through the packing room, shelves of little white boxes behind her)
Jess: We are running out of places to put these boxes, and it's almost all grandmas buying them for Christmas.
Jess (turning the phone on Diane): Diane, tell them what the boss just approved.
Diane (late 60s, holding a Palace Guard up to the camera): Okay, I've got four granddaughters, so I'm a little biased, but this is the best deal we've ever done. You buy three, you get two free, and that comes out to less than twenty dollars each.
(Kayla, at the packing table, pulls the pin; the siren fills the room and every head in the back turns; she pushes the pin back in)
Kayla: Sorry. That's what you're getting. It's as loud as an ambulance siren, right there on her keys, and it flashes so nobody can pretend they didn't see it.
Diane: Your granddaughter comes home for break, it goes in her stocking, and she goes back to school in January with it on her keys.
Jess: Why are we doing this? Look around.
(the camera swings down an aisle stacked to the ceiling)
Jess: The boss is a detective, and she said she doesn't want one girl going back to campus without one.
Maria (fanning five of them out in her hand): So that's five. One for every granddaughter, and if you've got more than five, I'm sorry, Grandma, you're getting ten.
Diane: Buy three, get two free, under twenty dollars each. And you get to be the grandma who gave her the one gift that actually mattered.
Jess: Order this week and it gets there before Christmas.
The boss (O.S., from the office): Who's filming in here?
Jess (whispering, walking faster): Click the link below and help us clear these shelves before she changes her mind.
