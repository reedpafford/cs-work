# RP12 — Don't let them pray for you (bodyguard reacts)
Brand: palace-guard · Persona: professional bodyguard (new) · Awareness: PA · Format: Reaction
Status: drafting
Parent: — · Lever: —

## Memo
Why: a viral TikTok ("Human trafficking, things to avoid: if someone at Walmart asks to pray for you, tell them no; don't take a Bible, it could have a tracker; don't touch what they hand you, it could be coated") already installed the fear in millions of mothers. We borrow the belief she already has and the plate that got the views, and finish the job the video didn't: it tells her what to avoid, never what to do when it happens anyway.
What: a professional bodyguard reacts to the viral video, says she's right, then shows what every one of her tips has in common and what actually stops it, which is the one thing his job is built on and her daughter doesn't have.
How: the viral TikTok as the swipe and the plate · the stitch/green-screen reaction shape · JC33's mechanism (attention, "noise is predator repellent") and RP1's product block in his voice.
Avatar: mothers and grandmothers 45 to 70 who share safety videos on Facebook and TikTok · Sub-avatar: the mother who already shared this exact video with her daughter.
Desire: her daughter has someone watching her back when she's alone in a parking lot, and she can do something about it today.
Angle: "She's right, but they don't always ask." Every tip in that video is the same move (get close, get her still, head down, hands full), and avoiding it only works if they ask first.
Mechanism: a bodyguard almost never fights; his job is attention. Nobody gets within arm's reach of a client without forty people looking, so they walk away. Her daughter doesn't have a bodyguard, so she carries the thing that does his job: one pin and every head in the lot turns.
Authority: twenty years as a professional bodyguard, plus a dad of girls (credibility and proximity in one speaker).
Beliefs to move, in order: the viral video is right, say no (agree, never debunk) → they don't always ask → the danger is the few seconds she's close and quiet → a bodyguard's real weapon is attention → she doesn't have one of me → this does my job → buy now.
Route: brand formula (persona story) in a Reaction format: the reaction is the lead, a story proves it, then the JC33 mechanism in his words and a new CTA built on the bodyguard (Reed, 2026-11-17: change up the CTA). Three hooks on one shared body.
