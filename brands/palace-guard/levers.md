## What a lever is
A lever is a repeatable move that takes a proven element from a winning ad and changes ONE dimension so a new audience sees it, or the same audience sees something new. It's arbitrage: the winner already did the hard market work. Ads are puzzle pieces; a lever swaps one piece and keeps the rest. The input-to-output asymmetry is the whole point: do less, take what works, get more out of it.

**The two caveats, both must hold.**
1. **Somebody sees something new.** Same audience + new ad, or new audience + same ad. Same audience + same ad is a repost, not a lever.
2. **No cannibalization.** Nothing already live in the CBO serving the exact same audience the exact same way. Same audience is fine if enough differs (structure, format, angle-in). Holistic; check the live ad sets before pulling.

**Hit rates, from the supplement account, unproven here:** an untapped lever ≈ one in two. Net-new ≈ one in four. Volume times quality; the flops are the cost of the hits. Objective, always: my read is a hypothesis, the market decides.

## The plan (Reed, 2026-09-16, sharpened from 09-14): unaware and problem-aware only, net-new first, levers second
There are almost no solution-aware buyers in this market (no daily pain, preventative purchase, people act only when an ad makes them). So the default job is one UA-to-PA long-form static or video, written harder than before, fewer of them; the engine is the belief that her daughter could be assaulted at any moment and the alarm stops it, and a story installs that belief best. SA and PDA are liquidation ads behind a winner (L4), never cold. **The Rosabella transposition (2026-09-16):** the first half of an unaware long-form static is product-agnostic, so an archived Rosabella winner keeps its front and only pivots the product in the back end. That's the cheapest net-new UA there is, and the source of new words for our own messaging. Candidates: the Rosabella Winners DB, `Archive` checked.
The biggest lever right now is not on the list below. It's **net-new winning messaging**: new hooks, new leads, problem-aware and unaware openings, in the cheap Authority style that converts, on the one proven avatar (parents and grandparents buying for their daughter). Quality over quantity, and still as much of it as we can put out. Solution-aware waits until the customer's psyche is better understood. The angles will reveal themselves as the tests pile up; nobody goes looking for them at the desk. So the levers below are what gets pulled on a winner once there is one, and the default job every week is the next net-new PA or UA ad.

## The levers, in order of how cheap they are to pull right now
### L1 · Language (Spanish)
Translate a winner. Same persona, same structure, same product block, only the language changes. The cheapest new audience there is; pull it on every winner. Runbook: `spanish.md`. Open: the Spanish lander, the Spanish voice.
- **Status:** untested here. First candidate: JC19 V3.

### L2 · Narrative
Keep the engine, the belief shifts, the persona and the audience; change the story's surface (the daughter's name, the school, who walked her home, who the authority is, the night it happened). The most evergreen lever, because stories are infinite. Works on the narrative personas (the mother, the female detective's cases); harder on educational scripts, which are angle-locked.
- **Proven engine to run it on:** JC19 (brutal confession hook → the story in order → the freeze inside it → the nurse names the red zone → the detective carries the mechanism and the product → the family bundle → the detective's grieving parents). The rules for the front are in `hooks.md` (Comprehension first, one thought per line, plain keywords).
- **Pulls so far:** JC20 (a real case in the female detective's voice), JC21 (the aftermath, years later). Both unread.

- **The JC31 engine, "new method, familiar place, almost got my daughter" (Reed, 2026-10-18: "this style kills").** The fixed part is JC31's back half: the freeze, the alarm first mentioned at the pull, the witnesses the place provides, the detective linking it to other girls with "why do you think," the origin told after, the product block, the place as the safety delusion, another mother's regret, and the share CTA. The parts to swap are the **method** (the trick), the **place**, the daughter and her errand, the witnesses, and the safeguard that fails. Swapping only the store is a revive (JC52), not a new ad. **Net new means a new method.** A method qualifies when (1) a mother can picture it in one sentence, (2) it uses a good habit we taught our girls (be polite, help people, get out after a fender-bender, check the tire), (3) she may already have seen it as a Facebook "stay alert" warning, so the ad confirms a rumor, and (4) the alarm lands before contact, with witnesses the place already has.

### L3 · Script structure
Same audience, same angle, new way of presenting it. The angle is what you say; the structure is how it's presented. Structures come from organic and competitor swipes (Researcher, rank 4), or get written new. Examples on this account: listicle, insider binary, confession narrative, real case, "imagine → it's real," the prehook second voice. Engine list: `hooks.md`.
- **When:** the audience is proven but the current structure is saturating, or a second bet on the same audience is wanted without cannibalizing.

### L4 · Awareness
Same pain and audience, a different awareness level. The play here is liquidation: when a PA video rips, make an SA, PDA or MA version so the people who saw it and didn't buy get a second, shorter ask. **Use the same avatar as the top-of-funnel winner**; the viewer recognizes the face and converts higher. A ripping PA video makes a dormant bottom-of-funnel ad start working too.
- **Account note:** SA as a fresh top-of-funnel opener has lost twice. Pull this lever behind a PA winner, never as a cold open.

### L5 · Angle (with gender folded in)
**Reed's definition for this brand:** an angle is one defined audience, a cohort exclusive from another, that hasn't seen the ad. Same format, same structure, near-same script; change who it's for (a pain point or a buyer) and it hits a fresh cohort. Gender is part of the angle here, not a separate lever: mother, father, grandmother, or the young woman herself are different cohorts.
- **Hypotheses to test, one brief each, only when the account has bandwidth:** parents → grandparents (JC22 drafted, parked until the lever is worth a test); parents → the daughter herself; mothers → fathers; college daughter → daughter who drives (JC16, unread); a different pain on the same hardware (the elderly-fall alarm, a separate audience and a separate pain).
- **The rule:** this lever is discovered in the account, not at the desk. An angle is proven when the same script on a new cohort buys. Until then it's a guess, and guesses get one brief.

### L6 · Format
Same script and angle, a different visual vehicle. Simplified for this brand: every talking head is **Authority**; every AI visual wrapper (claymation, skeleton, Lego, Pixar, voice-over animation) is **Animation**; then LFS, Reaction, Podcast. The bigger the visual and emotional jump, the bigger the new audience.
- **Status: parked.** Complex formats cost more, take longer and give the Editor more ways to miss. Everything stays Authority until the winning messaging is found. Then the move is: cheap talking-head test → the winner gets the expensive wrapper.

### L7 · Revive
Bring back winning messaging that has gone quiet, said again better, more plausibly, or in a newer format. Two clocks run it:
- **Age:** a winner that ran months ago and died can come back once the audience has turned over. **The account is not old enough yet.** Revisit when a winner is three or more months old.
- **Season:** the college angle is a red-zone product (move-in to Thanksgiving). It fades in summer and comes back every August. Same for drivers (holiday travel), and whatever else the account shows. When the season returns, revive the messaging that won in it last time.

## The use pattern
When a winner is ripping and Reed says "proliferate" or "build off this":
1. Name the asset, its persona, its structure, its audience and its awareness level.
2. Walk L1–L7 against it. For each: untapped, already covered, or would cannibalize (check the live CBO ad sets).
3. Name the specific move for each viable pull, one line each ("L1: translate JC19 V3, same voice, Mexican-leaning, native check first").
4. Rank by leverage, cheapest and highest-asymmetry first. Default order today: Language · Narrative · Script structure · Awareness (behind a PA winner) · Angle (one brief) · Format (parked) · Revive (not yet).
5. Hand Reed the menu. He picks. Each pick becomes a brief via `brief-creation.md`, and the brief names the lever, so the lever's hit rate gets measured here.
On-demand only. I don't lever-map every brief; I map a winner when Reed asks.

## Never
- Pull a lever onto an unproven ad. A lever multiplies a winner; on a loser it multiplies nothing.
- Run two levers at once on the same asset and call it one test. One dimension changes per pull, or the read is worthless.
- Treat the supplement hit rates as this brand's. They're a prior, not a fact, until this brand has ten pulls.
