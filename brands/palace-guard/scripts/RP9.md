## Memo
Why: Every brief so far assumes the daughter has a phone. The kid who's too young for one is the one nobody can find, and the grandparent buying for that kid is the 65+ buyer who bought 19 of 28 on JC10 V2. It runs in the holiday window, so the sale is real and the story is happening right now.
What: A mall Santa walks two grandkids out the back to a car while their grandma's stuck buying the photo, and the pink alarm on the girl's backpack is the only reason both of them come home.
How: RP5 (the movie shape: the cold open, the parent's side intercut, the product facts at the detective's table, the V.O. close) · JC35 (the younger-kids beats and mechanism) · JC33 (the evidence-bag line).
Avatar: the grandparent or parent of a child too young for a phone · Sub-avatar: the grandma who takes the grandkids out on her own at the holidays.
Desire: keep them safe without handing a seven-year-old a phone.
Angle: The kid with no phone is the one you can't find, and this is how she makes you find her.
Mechanism: They work on distraction and speed: pull the grown-up's eyes away for two minutes and get the kids off the first spot, and after that they're gone. A kid crying at the mall at Christmas doesn't turn one head, but a 130 dB siren turns every one, and it's how a kid with no phone gets found. Pink, so she actually carries it; sturdy; no app, no phone.
Authority: the detective at the kitchen table, whose daughters in college carry one, because he's seen the cheap ones in evidence bags.
Route: Movie (craft/formats/movie.md). Offer: the holiday sale, buy three get two free, under twenty dollars each, thirty-day guarantee.
