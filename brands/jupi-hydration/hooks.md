## Banked (unused, ready if a version needs a new hook)
**JRP4, the three-clone hydration comparison (2026-11-24); each runs into the body at "Well, with the packets…"**
- **The clones argue** (the Bissell fountain setup): Water: "I drink a gallon a day, so I'm definitely more hydrated than you." Packets: "A gallon? I put a packet in every bottle." Water: "Well, I'm so hydrated I'm in the bathroom every twenty minutes." Mix: "That's actually the problem. So which one of us is really hydrated?" The bathroom brag is the joke and the belief shift's setup at once.
- **Each clone states his symptom:** "I drink plain water, and I'm still bloated by dinner. I drink electrolyte packets, and I still crash at three. And I drink this anti-bloating hydration mix. Which one of us is actually hydrated?" She picks a clone by what she feels; gives the winner away early, like the swipe did.
- **How X vs how Y** (JRP1's shape): "How tired people hydrate versus how people with endless energy hydrate. What's the difference?" The desire in the first line; one guy with labels, not three clones.
