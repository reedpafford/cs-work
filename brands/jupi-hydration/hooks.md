## Rules

**Rule (Reed, JRP3, 2026-11-24): the hook says the problem by its word.** "To fix my stubborn bloating," not "so my jeans still button by dinner." The picture can come in the body.
**Rule (Reed, JRP3): in a two-person skit, the buyer opens.** He answers; she's who the viewer sees herself in.
