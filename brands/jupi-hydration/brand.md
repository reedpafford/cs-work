## Avatar
**One ICP per avatar as its home base; crossover happens on its own. A demographic is not an ICP:** "women 35+" gives zero scripts; "a 47-year-old who unbuttons her jeans at her desk by 5pm and has been told twice it's just stress" gives ten. Niching harder inside a lane makes the avatar stronger.
- **F1 menopause-era woman (45–60), the largest and most underserved:** bloats from foods that never bothered her, weight around the middle, run down; dismissed ("it's just your age"), betrayed by her body.
- **F2 stressed career woman or caregiver (35–55):** stomach in knots, stress bloat on barely any food, sick the moment she rests; feels punished for working hard.
- **F3 always-sick woman (40–65):** catches everything, two weeks to recover; quietly afraid of becoming fragile.
- **F4 sensitive-gut woman (30–55):** trigger-food list, scans menus, plans life around her stomach; wants food freedom.
- **F5 depleted-gut woman (35–60):** "My stomach hasn't been right since those antibiotics."
- **F6 desk-life woman (35–55):** the 5pm bloat, unbuttoning jeans by evening, "I eat a salad and still bloat"; wants low effort, sceptical of overhauls.
- **F7 run-down mum (30–48):** catches every bug the kids bring home; won't buy for herself, will buy to stay strong for her family.
- **M1 bloated midlife man (45–65):** the belly that isn't just beer; won't touch anything feminine or medical; his wife is often the buyer.
- **M2 stressed working man (35–55):** performance framing, never vulnerability.
- **M3 older man slowing down (55–70):** wants to stay capable and independent.
- **M4 fit-but-bloated man (30–48):** trains hard, eats clean, still bloated; body as a project.
All four male lanes are wide open.
