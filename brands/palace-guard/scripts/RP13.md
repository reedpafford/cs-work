I've been doing this for eleven years, and I've got a daughter of my own, so I'm gonna tell you what we don't put in the welcome packet.

But it's what we don't see that keeps me up at night.
While we're breaking up a party on one side of campus, there's a girl walking back to her dorm alone on the other side, and nobody's watching that walk.
And that's when most of these attacks happen.
