# RP28 — The one I scrolled past (the movie)
Brand: palace-guard · Persona: mother (Linda, lead, her V.O. carries the back half and the close) · Awareness: PA · Format: Movie
Status: drafting
Parent: — · Lever: —
Cast: Linda (the mom, early 50s) · Greg (the dad, mid 50s, brushes it off) · Kate (20, a junior, home for winter break) · Abby (17, the younger sister, leaves for college next fall) · the guy at the door, one per hook: Tyler (the nice guy from her class) · a man in a hoodie (a stranger) · Josh (her friend since freshman year) · Ryan (her ex)

## Memo
Why: RP17 sold the save; nothing of ours has sold the regret of not buying. The mother watching our ads is scrolling past them, so this movie shows her doing exactly that, the same night it happens. · What: the last night before winter break, Kate is assaulted behind her dorm door while her mom, two hours away, scrolls past an ad for an alarm like she always does; Kate comes home for the holidays changed, her dad calls her moody, her mom finds out, and the rest is the mom's guilt in voice-over, imagining the version where she bought it, which the viewer sees. · How: RP17's shape (the mom leads, the dad dismisses, the V.O. close), four hooks that test the level of the approach (the nice guy, a stranger, her friend, her ex), all grounded, all ending behind the same door, then a back half that's mostly Linda's internal V.O. over what is cut against what would have been.
Avatar: mothers 45 to 65 of a daughter in college · Sub-avatar: the mom who has seen these ads and scrolled past, because she didn't have a reason.
Desire: never having to live with "what if."
Angle: she's scrolled past these ads a hundred times because they were for other people's daughters; the belief shift is that it can happen to yours. Kate doesn't get her life back, and the mom's warning to the viewer is don't make the same assumption.
Mechanism: attention; one pull at the door and the whole building knows. Shown in the would-have, never explained.
Authority: none; the would-have sequence is the proof.
Route: Movie (craft/formats/movie.md), no time jumps, no expert; Linda's internal V.O. runs from the moment she knows to the end and turns to the viewer for the close.

## V1 - The nice guy

(Kate and Tyler walk up to the door laughing; her keys hang off her finger, a plain ring with her student ID)

Tyler: Your roommate already go home for break?

Tyler: So you've got the place to yourself tonight.

Tyler: Let me come up. Just for a little.

(he catches the door above her head before it can close)
Tyler: Come on. Don't be like that.
Kate: Tyler, no. Seriously.
(he follows her inside, and the door swings shut behind them)

## Body
### Script

[SCENE 1 — Linda and Greg's bedroom, the same minute]
(Greg asleep; Linda in bed, her phone the only light)
(she types to Kate: Can't wait to see you tomorrow! Dad's getting you at the airport at noon. Love you.)
(Delivered)
(she scrolls her feed; an ad comes up, a girl's keys with a little pink alarm on the ring)
(her thumb doesn't even slow down; she scrolls past it to a recipe)
(she sets the phone on the nightstand and turns off the lamp)

[SCENE 2 — the airport, the next day, noon]
(Kate comes through the doors in an oversized hoodie, the hood up, sunglasses, no makeup)
Greg: There she is!
(he wraps her in a big hug; she goes stiff, her arms stay at her sides)
(Linda watches over his shoulder)
Linda: Hi, baby. You look exhausted.
Kate: Finals.

[SCENE 3 — the car, on the highway home]
Greg: So. How's the boyfriend situation? Anybody we need to meet?
(Kate stares out the window)
Kate: Can we not.
Greg (laughing): Okay, okay. Touchy.
(Linda looks at her in the visor mirror)

[SCENE 4 — the living room, that night, decorating the tree]
(Christmas music; Abby on a step stool hanging ornaments; Greg untangling a strand of lights)
Abby: Kate, you want to do the star? You always do the star.
Kate (from the stairs): You do it.
(she goes up to her room; her door shuts)
Abby: What's wrong with her?
Greg: She's twenty. She's moody.
Linda: She hasn't eaten all day, Greg.
Greg: It's probably her time of the month. Leave her alone, she'll snap out of it.
Abby: She's not even going to Megan's party. She never misses Megan's party.
Greg: College kids come home and sleep for three days, Lin. It's what they do.
(Linda looks up the stairs at the closed door)

[SCENE 5 — the next two days, no words]
(two in the afternoon; Kate's door still shut)
(the shower running; Linda in the hallway checks the time on her phone; forty minutes)
(dinner; Kate in long sleeves, pushing her food around the plate)
(Greg reaches past her for the salt, and she flinches)
(Greg doesn't notice; Linda does)

[SCENE 6 — Kate's bedroom, that night]
(Linda knocks with two mugs of cocoa)
Linda: Can I come in?
(Kate on her bed in the dark, the hood up, her knees to her chest)
(Linda sets the mugs on the nightstand and sits on the edge of the bed)
Linda: Sweetie. What happened?
Kate: Nothing. I'm just tired.
Linda: Kate. I'm your mom.
(Kate opens her mouth, and nothing comes out)
Linda: Did somebody hurt you?
(Kate nods, and she breaks)
(Linda pulls her in; Kate sobs into her shoulder)
(over Kate's shoulder, Linda's eyes land on the desk: Kate's keys, a plain ring, her student ID, nothing else)
(at the bottom of the stairs, Greg stands still with the strand of lights in his hands, listening)

[SCENE 7 — the same night, and the days after: Linda's V.O.]
(Linda holds Kate in the dark, rocking her)
Linda (V.O.): She didn't have to say it. I knew the second she nodded.
(later; the kitchen, Linda alone at the table, her phone the only light)
(she scrolls; the same kind of ad comes up, a pink alarm on a girl's keys)
(this time her thumb stops)
Linda (V.O.): I've scrolled past those ads so many times. The little alarms on a keychain. I always figured they were for other people's daughters.
Linda (V.O.): Girls who walk home alone in some big city. Not mine. Mine goes to a nice school two hours away, she has a roommate, and she calls me every Sunday.
(Greg in the doorway in his robe; he sees the screen, and he doesn't say a word)
Linda (V.O.): I never thought it would be my daughter. I don't think any mom does.
(what would have happened: Kate at her dorm door that night, her keys in her hand, and this time a pink alarm on the ring)
Linda (V.O.): Now all I can do is picture her at that door with one on her keys.
(a hand reaches for her)
(she pulls the pin)
(the siren rings off the walls; the strobe flashes white down the hallway)
(doors open up and down the hall; a girl in pajamas leans out)
Girl: Hey! What's going on out there?
(he backs away and runs for the stairs)
(Kate on the phone in the hallway, shaking, but okay)
Kate (on the phone): Mom, I'm okay. I'm okay.
(back to what is: Kate asleep in Linda's lap in the dark, still in her hoodie)
Linda (V.O.): I don't know if it would have changed everything. But she would have had a chance, and I didn't give her one.
(three in the morning; Linda at the kitchen table, reading on her phone)
Linda (V.O.): I read everything I could find after that. One in five girls on a campus like hers gets assaulted before she graduates, and most of the time it's somebody she knows.
Linda (V.O.): He's counting on a closed door, where nobody can hear her. One pull of that pin, and the whole building does.
(Christmas morning; Abby and Greg on the floor by the tree, wrapping paper everywhere)

(what would have been: Kate on the couch in her pajamas, laughing, throwing a bow at Abby)

Linda (V.O.): Christmas morning, she didn't come down.

Linda (V.O.): Greg hasn't called her moody since. I don't think he's forgiven himself. I know I haven't.

Abby: What's this for?
(Linda can't answer; she closes Abby's fingers around it)
Linda: Just keep it on there. Promise me.
