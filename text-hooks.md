> Sources mined: text-hook-rules · brief-finalize-sop (copy-verbatim rule) · JC13 ("Why your DAUGHTER CAN'T SCREAM...😳 (from a detective)").

## What a text hook is
The burned-in plate. Read in under a second, sound off. Its only job is stopping the scroll. It does not need to be grammatical. Reed: "if you can really just combine keywords and it's somewhat coherent, it will work."
✅ `NEW Predator ABDUCTION Method…‼️` (Reed, JC30, one plate on three story hooks: the promise of the warning hook, the keyword, no scene) · ✅ `5 things for COLLEGE DAUGHTER SAFETY...😳` · ✅ `Your COLLEGE DAUGHTER is in the RED ZONE...🚨` · ✅ `Why your DAUGHTER CAN'T SCREAM...😳 (from a detective)`

## The method, per variation
1. Read that variation's **spoken hook only**. Ignore the rest of the script.
2. Pull the 2–3 nouns a scrolling mother must see to know it's for her (DAUGHTER · COLLEGE · RED ZONE · SCREAM · PEPPER SPRAY).
3. CAPS those words, lowercase the fewest possible words around them.
4. End with `...` + one emoji (😳 🚨 😰 are the house set).
5. **Check:** does the plate promise exactly what the first ten seconds deliver? If the plate says something the script reveals at minute two, rewrite.

## Rules
- **Simplest sentence structure: noun, verb, noun.** Who did what to whom, in three or four words, then the dots and the emoji. ❌ `Daughter ASSAULTED by COP…😳` (passive, four words before the picture lands) → ✅ `Cop ASSAULTED college girl…😳` (Reed, JC20, 2026-09-14). The plate is a sentence a half-watching mother reads in one glance, not a headline.
- **Align to the spoken hook, never the middle.** A listicle hook gets a listicle plate. A story hook gets a story plate. The #1 failure is pulling a mid-script reveal (the freeze, the red zone name) into the plate: it spends the ad's best moment for free AND breaks the promise. (JC10, 2026-08-31)
- **Tailored per variation.** Three variations = three groups of options, each aligned to that variation's opening line. (JC16)
- **Plain lines, no slashes.** Never `If your DAUGHTER DRIVES / watch this...`. Chip-splitting to ≤28 chars is the Editor's layout problem. (JC16)

## Delivery shape
```
V1 — "[spoken hook]"
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...
V2 — "[spoken hook]"
...
```
No preamble, no explanation per line. If Reed asks "give me more," give more of the same engine, not a new engine.
