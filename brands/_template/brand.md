# <Brand> — brand
> One brand's pocket. Filled on "new brand" from what Reed pastes, gaps asked in one short list. Only true facts here (the ads can dramatize later, CLAUDE.md principle 6). Questions come from `craft/playbook.md` §3. Leave a heading as `?` until it's known.

## Product
- **Physical:** what it is, what it's made of.
- **Functional:** what it does (people buy this).
- **How it works,** then the same in sixth-grade words.
- **Features → benefits → desires** (keep asking "why?").
- **Hidden mechanism:** what it does that isn't advertised · what it has that competitors don't · "if it worked too well, what would customers complain about?" · how a customer explains it to a friend.
- **Mechanism type:** new / unspoken / renamed, and the one line that names it.

## Offer
Price as spoken, bundle, guarantee (worded exactly), scarcity, the link.

## Desire
The desires it can believably deliver, scored on scope, urgency, staying power. Seasonal peaks.

## Avatar
- **Core avatar** (the group most likely to buy), one desire, in their words.
- **Sub-avatars:** experience · emotion · behavior · demographic (last).
- **Labels** they call themselves.

## Customer language
Their words, quoted, with the source: complaints, "I want / I need," what they call the problem and the product.

## Beliefs and objections
"People believe X" lines. Objections by type: category ("this doesn't work"), claim ("too good to be true"), avatar ("not for me").

## Market
Awareness (where most buyers are) · sophistication stage · competitor claims tallied from the ad library · the enemy (an approach, a belief, a competitor) · the hole competitors won't say.

## Authority available
Credentials, founder, research, experience, borrowed, visual.

## Personas
Who speaks in the ads, and each one's register: three words they'd say, one they never would.

## Compliance
Platform landmines for this brand beyond the house word and image rules (CLAUDE.md principle 8): claims, words, images.

## Brand formula
`?` until a brief wins. Then: the structure that won, its brief, and what it proves.

## Product blocks
`?` until a brief is final. Then: the newest product block per persona, with its brief.
